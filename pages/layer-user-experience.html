User Surface & Agency

Everything the user actually sees, taps, and controls: how answers arrive, how much the AI does on its own, and how people push back or give direction.

01The Idea

What is it?

The user surface and agency layer is everything between the AI's raw output and the person reading it. The answer does not just appear all at once: it streams in token by token (a token is a small piece the AI works in: roughly a word, or a slice of an image or sound), so it feels alive and fast. Alongside the answer, the interface shows where the information came from (citations), lets you copy or retry with one tap, and gives you a way to say whether it helped. Each of those pieces is a deliberate design choice, not an accident.

03A Real Example

What each piece actually does.

Streaming
ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all stream: they send the answer token by token the moment each word is ready, instead of waiting until the full reply is done. This makes a 10-second reply feel like a conversation, not a page load.
Citations
Perplexity AI shows numbered source pills next to each sentence. Tap one and it opens the exact page. This lets you check the answer and builds trust, because you can see where it came from.
Copy / Retry
Copy puts the answer on the clipboard in one tap. Retry sends the same question again to get a different attempt, useful when the first answer missed the point.
Thumbs Up / Down
Every AI product collects this signal. Teams use it to find answers that users disliked, where it feeds into training and evaluation that shape future answers. One tap turns into a data point teams can act on.
04Tradeoffs

Design choices, each with a cost.

Low High
User control
Build complexity
Feedback signal
Perceived speed
Streaming (progressive disclosure) dramatically raises perceived speed but requires a persistent connection. Feedback thumbs collect sparse, noisy signal: most users never press them, and a thumbs-down could mean "wrong answer," "wrong tone," or just "I am frustrated." Citations need a retrieval system (like RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation) that tracks sources, adding infrastructure cost.
05Inside This Layer

What do you want to build?

Where you meet the AI

Want a turn-by-turn chat window or voice interface? → Conversational Surfaces
Want AI to appear inside an existing tool without breaking flow? → Inline / Embedded Surfaces
Want the AI to work in the background and surface results when done? → Background / Agentic Surfaces

How answers show up

Want the answer to appear word by word so it feels fast? → Streaming & Progressive Disclosure
Want the AI to ask a follow-up when it is not sure what you meant? → Clarification & Disambiguation Surfaces
Want to collect thumbs, ratings, or corrections from users? → Feedback Capture Surfaces

Staying in control

Want a graceful path from AI to a human agent? → Handoff & Escalation
Want the AI to pause and ask before doing something consequential? → Approval Gates
Want to set how much the AI can do without asking each time? → Autonomy Levels & Guardrails
