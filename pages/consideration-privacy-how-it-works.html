Privacy, Security & Governance

Personal details belong to people, not to models. Keep them private, separated, and traceable.

01The Idea

What is it?

Privacy means the system finds and strips personal details before storing a message or sending it to the model, and keeps one person's data from ever reaching another person's session.

03A Real Example

How a support tool applies this.

1
User sends a message
A customer types freely, often including their name, order number, or phone number without thinking.
2
Personal-info scanner runs before anything else
A pattern matcher finds names, emails, phone numbers, and government IDs. Each is replaced with a neutral tag like [EMAIL] before the text moves forward.
3
Redacted message goes to the model
The AI sees the topic and intent (password reset), but never the real personal details. It can answer helpfully without needing them.
4
Original is stored only in a protected log
If needed for audit, the unredacted version sits in an access-controlled store, not mixed in with training data or conversation history.
04Tradeoffs

The price you pay.

lowhigh
Engineering effort
Latency added
Missed detections
Context lost to the model
Missed detections are the real risk, not the latency. A scanner that lets one ID slip through is worse than one that runs a touch slower.
