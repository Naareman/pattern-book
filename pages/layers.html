Layers

The parts an AI is built from, top (what people use) down to the raw data.

10 User Experience(what you see and click)The screen you talk to: the chat, live answers, sources, and your feedback. → 9 Application Patterns(the kind of task)The kind of job you give it: answer, pull out facts, shorten, sort, make, choose, or do tasks itself. → 8 Orchestration & Agents(planning the steps)The plan for the steps it follows and the helpers it uses. → 7 Single-Call Control(asking one question well)What you set up for one question: how you ask, how it thinks, how the answer looks. → 6 Stateful Memory(what it remembers)What the AI remembers from earlier messages and past chats. → 5 Stateless Retrieval(looking things up)How it looks something up right then to answer you, then forgets it. → 4 Structural Composition(combining answers)How a few AI answers are combined, picked from, or used to check each other. → 3 Training & Adaptation(teaching the model)How the AI is taught and adjusted before you ever use it. → 2 Serving & Infrastructure(running it for everyone)How the AI is kept running fast and always on for everyone. → 1 Data & Artifact Lifecycle(the raw material)All the information and saved files the AI is built from. →
