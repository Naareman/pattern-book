Summarization Service

Turns long documents into short, faithful summaries at scale.

01The Idea

What is it?

When a document is too long to read in one go, you split it into smaller pieces, summarize each piece in parallel, then summarize those summaries into one final answer.

Application patterns Single-call control Composition Cost & latency
02See It Work

Watch the map-reduce.

A long document is split into chunks. Each chunk is summarized in parallel (the "map" step). Then all chunk summaries are combined into one final summary (the "reduce" step).

Input Document
Annual Climate Report 2024 (42 pages)
~28,000 tokens total. Model context limit: 8,000 tokens per call. Split into 6 chunks.
1 Split: divide into chunks waiting
Chunk 1
Pages 1-7: Executive summary and global temperature trends.
Temperatures rose 1.4C above pre-industrial baseline.
Chunk 2
Pages 8-14: Ocean data, sea-level rise, and Arctic ice extent.
Sea levels up 4.5 mm; Arctic summer ice at record low.
Chunk 3
Pages 15-21: Extreme weather events and attribution studies.
Heatwaves 5x more likely; flood severity increased in 60% of basins.
Chunk 4
Pages 22-28: Emissions by sector and country-level commitments.
Energy sector still 34% of emissions; 80 countries updated their climate pledges.
Chunk 5
Pages 29-35: Adaptation strategies and investment gaps.
$340bn adaptation gap; coastal infrastructure most underfunded.
Chunk 6
Pages 36-42: Policy recommendations and 2030 targets.
Recommends 45% emissions cut by 2030 to stay under 1.5C.
2 Map: summarize each chunk in parallel waiting
6 calls fire at once, one per chunk. Each call gets a prompt (the text you send the AI): "Summarize this section faithfully in 2 sentences." The AI returns a mini-summary for each chunk.
3 Reduce: combine into one final summary waiting
Final Summary
The 2024 Climate Report documents a 1.4C rise above pre-industrial levels, record-low Arctic ice, and a 4.5 mm sea-level increase. Extreme weather has intensified sharply. Despite 80 countries updating their climate commitments, a $340bn adaptation funding gap remains. The report calls for a 45% emissions reduction by 2030 to hold warming under 1.5C.
03A Real Example

How a real one runs.

A legal team processes hundreds of contracts a week. Tap each step to walk through the pipeline.

Ingest
A 60-page contract arrives as a PDF. The system extracts text: at roughly 900 tokens per page, that is 54,000 tokens total, far above any single-call limit.
