Playbook for when things go wrong

A written sequence the team runs the moment the service starts to misbehave: who calls it, who fixes it, who tells the customer.

01What It Is

A page everyone reads at 3 a.m.

An incident playbook is the short, plain document the team reaches for when a target starts slipping. It names the roles (someone runs the response, someone fixes the system, someone talks to customers), the first three checks to run, the fallbacks to flip, and the way to write down what happened after. The aim is to move the team from panic to method in the first five minutes.

03Where It Fits

How it relates to nearby ideas.

Pairs with
How much can break before you act. The budget tells the team to reach for the playbook. The playbook says what to do once they reach for it.
Feeds it
Traces and metrics from the observability layer. A playbook with no live data is a script the team cannot follow.
04When It Matters

When to reach for it.

  • The service is live for paying customers and a bad hour is felt by the business.
  • The team is on call and the on-call rotation includes people who joined this quarter.
  • You depend on outside model providers and you need a rehearsed move for when one goes down.
