Pattern

Chain-of-Thought

01The Idea

What is it?

Chain-of-Thought is just one line you add to your prompt, like "Let's think step by step."
That one line gets the model to work the problem out before answering, and the working is what gets it right.

02How it Works

On the whiteboard.

Watch the model work one problem out, step by step:

Question 3 baskets, each with 6 apples. I eat 4 apples. How many are left?
Press Play, and watch the model write its working out.
reads the question, left to right, as tokens
1
works out a first result
2
carries that result forward
3
Answer 14 apples are left.
The answer falls out of the last line, not a guess. Without the steps, models often jump to a wrong number.
