Streaming & progressive disclosure

The reply appears as it is being written, instead of arriving as one finished block.

01What is it?

Watching the answer get written.

Instead of waiting for the AI to finish thinking, the screen shows each word the moment it is produced. You see the reply growing in front of you, like someone typing. The first word arrives in a fraction of a second, so the wait feels much shorter, and you can start reading right away. If the answer is going wrong, you can stop it early.

03Where it fits

How it sits next to other pieces.

What it is
A look-and-feel choice for the reply. The words show up live, the moment they are ready.
Pairs with
A quiet check on the words as they arrive, so a broken reply can be caught early. That check is a separate tool that lives under the hood.
Not this
A loading spinner that hides everything until the answer is done. That is the opposite choice.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • Chat-like products, where any delay over a second feels broken.
  • Long answers, where waiting in silence for the whole thing is painful.
  • Anywhere a stop button matters, so the person can cut a bad reply short.
