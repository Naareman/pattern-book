Stateful Memory · What it remembers

Episodic memory

Time-stamped moments. "On Tuesday you said X" or "last week you booked Y." Each one is a single thing that happened.

01What it is

A diary of moments, not traits.

Episodic memory stores individual time-stamped events so the model can surface a specific past moment when a later question triggers it.

02An example

Tap a moment to see how it comes back.

Past events the model has saved
  • 12 March
    Booked a flight to Rome for 22 May.
  • 04 April
    Mentioned a dentist appointment moved to next Friday.
  • 19 April
    Said you finished reading "Pachinko" and loved it.
Three weeks later you ask: "What hotels should I book?" The model recalls the Rome flight and answers "For your Rome trip on 22 May, here are some options near Termini..."
03Where it fits

How it sits next to nearby ideas.

Not the same as
Your profile (name, allergies, tone). The profile is steady traits. Events are dated moments that happened once and may not repeat.
Works with
The trigger that decides when to surface an event. Saving an event is one half. Knowing the right moment to bring it back is the other.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • You want the model to follow up on something you mentioned days or weeks ago.
  • The task involves a story over time: a project, a trip, a treatment plan, a course.
  • The model needs to say "you told me on Tuesday that..." with the date and the exact thing.
