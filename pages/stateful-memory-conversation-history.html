Conversation memory

The running record of what you and the model just said to each other in this chat.

01What it is

A scroll of the last few turns.

Conversation memory is the running list of messages in the current chat, kept so each reply can refer back to what was just said, with the oldest turns dropped or summarized when the list grows too long.

02An example

A short chat that builds on itself.

Turn 1 of 3
You
I am planning a trip to Tokyo in April.
Model
Cherry blossom season. Are you looking for food spots, sights, or both?
Turn 1. The model has heard about Tokyo and April. Both lines are now part of the history.
03Where it fits

How it sits next to nearby ideas.

Sits next to
Things you said about yourself (your name, your preferences). Those live longer than one chat. The history here is only this conversation.
What happens when it overflows
When the chat gets too long, older turns get shortened into a summary or dropped. The summary stays in the running history so the thread still makes sense.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • Any chat where later questions only make sense given the earlier ones (follow-ups, clarifications).
  • The model needs to refer to something you said two or three turns ago.
  • Long chats where you have to decide what to keep versus what to drop.
