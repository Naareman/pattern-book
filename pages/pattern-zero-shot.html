Zero-Shot

Just describe what you want. The model figures it out from what it already knows, with no examples needed.

01The Idea

What is it?

Zero-shot is just describing what you want, with no examples, and letting the AI work it out from what it already knows.

02See It Work

Plain words in, answer out.

Pick a task and watch the model answer from training alone. No examples were given.

Zero-Shot (this page) No examples. Faster. Cheaper. Less control over exact format.
Few-Shot 1-5 examples included. More control over output style, but longer prompt and higher cost.

Sources
  • Brown et al. (2020). Language Models are Few-Shot Learners. Introduced GPT-3 and established zero-shot as a baseline; showed models can follow plain-language instructions without examples.
  • Kojima et al. (2022). Large Language Models are Zero-Shot Reasoners. Showed that adding "Let's think step by step" to a zero-shot prompt dramatically improves multi-step reasoning, pointing to the boundary where zero-shot needs help.
