Conversational Assistant

A chat interface that remembers what you said earlier and answers helpfully, turn by turn.

01The Idea

What is it?

A Conversational Assistant is a chat where the AI remembers the whole conversation so far, because the system quietly hands the running transcript back to it on every reply.

User experience Stateful Memory Single-call control
02See It Work

Watch it remember.

Each new reply streams in word by word (a token at a time). Notice how the third answer explicitly references what you said two turns earlier.

A token is a small piece the AI works in: roughly a word, or a slice of an image or sound.

The price you pay.

Cost and wait time grow with conversation length: every turn re-sends the full transcript, so a 30-turn chat costs more than a 3-turn one. The fix (summarising old turns) saves tokens but risks forgetting specific details. That is the central tradeoff of rolling-window memory.
