Considerations

What every part of an AI must get right, whatever the layer.

Evaluation(is it right?)Checking the AI is actually correct, not just confident. → Safety(keeping it safe)Stopping harmful, unsafe, or abusive answers. → Privacy(protecting data)Keeping people's personal data safe and handled right. → Observability(seeing what it did)Being able to look back at what the AI did and why. → Cost(cheap, fast, steady)Keeping it affordable, fast, and dependable. → Multimodal(beyond text)Handling images, sound, and video, not just words. → Real-time(feeling instant)Answering fast, word by word, so it feels live. →
