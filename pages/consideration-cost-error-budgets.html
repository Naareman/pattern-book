How much can break before you act

Give the service a monthly allowance of mistakes. Spend it on ship and learn. When it runs out, slow down and shore up.

01What It Is

A monthly budget for failure.

An error budget is the room left under the target. If the promise is 99.9 percent uptime in a 30 day month, the team has about 43 minutes of allowed downtime. Each outage, each slow day, each broken release spends some of it. When most of the budget is gone, the rule is simple: freeze risky changes, pay back the debt. Graceful degradation is the friend: a smaller model, a stale answer, a cached page, anything that keeps the user moving while the team fixes the real thing.

How it relates to nearby ideas.

04When It Matters

When to reach for it.

  • The team needs a clear, agreed signal for when to slow down on releases and when to keep shipping.
  • An upstream model provider has flaky days, and the team wants a rule for when to fall back to a smaller model.
  • Leadership wants a steady promise to customers without grinding shipping to a halt.
