Checking progress along the way

Training takes weeks. Every so often you save a snapshot of the model and quiz it on a set of held-back questions to see whether it is still getting better.

01What is it?

A pop quiz on the model as it trains.

At regular intervals you freeze a copy of the model (a "snapshot"), score it on a small bank of questions the training never saw, and keep the snapshot that scores highest, so you do not accidentally ship a version that has overworked itself and become worse.

03Where it fits

Where it sits in the training story.

It runs alongside
Every other step in this layer. Whether the model is doing its first long read or being tuned for behavior, snapshots and a held-back question bank keep training honest.
Not the same as
The final test you run on a shipped model. That is for buyers and users. This is the team's own thermometer during training.
04When it matters

When to take it seriously.

  • Training is long enough that the model can overshoot and get worse if you do not check.
  • The held-back questions have to be kept truly separate; if the model studied them, the quiz lies.
  • You want to compare two training recipes fairly: same snapshots, same questions, then look at the curve.
