Customer Support Agent

A real product: the agent reads your help guides, answers customer questions in plain language, and passes the hard ones to a human.

Knowledge & Memory Application Patterns
01The Idea

What is it?

A customer support agent is an AI that knows your product through its help articles. When a customer asks a question, the agent finds the right article, pulls the relevant paragraph, and writes a plain-language reply. When the question is too complex or the agent is not sure, it stops and sends the conversation to a human. No guessing. No hallucinating policies it does not have.
