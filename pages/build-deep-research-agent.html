Deep Research Agent

A long-running team of sub-agents that turn one question into a fully cited report, with time to think.

01The Idea

What is it?

A Deep Research Agent turns one question into a long, cited report by breaking it down, sending sub-agents off to research, and synthesising what they bring back.

Default in 2026 OpenAI Deep Research or Anthropic Computer Use. Decompose query into 5-15 sub-questions; assign each to a sub-agent; aggregate; cite every claim. Plan to wait 5-20 minutes.

3 Chairman: synthesise the cited report waiting
The chairman reads all five findings together and writes one coherent recommendation with numbered citations back to each sub-agent's sources.

Recommendation: buy and customise. Eight of eleven comparable European banks have already chosen this path[5], and the economics support it: leading vendors charge £200-450k/yr[2] versus £1.4-2.1M year-one to build in-house[3].

The regulatory floor is the same either way: compliance-review AI is classified as high-risk under the EU AI Act Annex III, requiring conformity assessment and a human-in-the-loop checkpoint[1]. Buying shifts the model-risk evidence burden to a vendor with an existing AI Act file; building keeps full audit-trail control but full liability[4].

Suggested path: 90-day vendor bake-off (Hummingbird, ComplyAdvantage Mesh, Greenlite) with a parallel internal prototype to validate that customisation hooks meet your control catalogue.

04Tradeoffs

The price you pay.

lowhigh
Depth of report
Wait time
Cost per query
Coordination complexity
Drift / hallucination risk
Deepest possible output at the highest possible wait time. Every sub-agent is another LLM context, another set of citations to verify, another chance for drift. The shape only earns its cost when the answer needs to be a report, not a paragraph.

06When to use vs alternatives

When this vs that.

vs Agentic RAG
Pick Agentic RAG when the output is a single grounded answer in seconds. Pick Deep Research when the output is a multi-section cited report and the user accepts a long wait.
vs Tool-using Agent
Pick a Tool-using Agent when the goal is to act (run code, book a flight, file a ticket). Pick Deep Research when the goal is to know: pure reading and writing, no side effects on the world.
vs Single-call answer
Use a single call when one prompt can plausibly carry the whole answer. Reach for Deep Research only when the question needs multi-hour depth, fan-out across many sources, and every claim has to be citable.
