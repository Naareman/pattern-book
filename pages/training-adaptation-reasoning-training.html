Training it to reason step by step

Let the model write out long working notes on hard problems, reward only the attempts that reach the right answer, and bake that habit into its weights.

01What is it?

Reward the working, not just the answer.

The model is given problems where the right answer can be checked automatically (a math result, a passing test, a verified proof); it tries many times, writes out its working each time, and the training keeps the routes that arrived at the correct answer, so over time it gets better at the kind of patient, step-by-step thinking those problems need.

02An example

One problem, three attempts, one rewarded.

Math problem (answer can be checked)
If 3x + 7 = 22, what is x?
×
Attempt 1
"Take 7 from 22: that's 15. Divide by 3: x = 4." Arithmetic slip. Wrong.
✓
Attempt 2 · rewarded
"Subtract 7 from both sides: 3x = 15. Divide both sides by 3: x = 5. Check: 3(5) + 7 = 22. Correct."
×
Attempt 3
"Try x = 6. 3(6) + 7 = 25. Too big. Try x = 5. 3(5) + 7 = 22. Yes, but answer is 6 by inspection." Contradicts itself. Wrong.
Only attempt 2 gets the reward. Repeat across millions of problems and the model gets steadier at the kind of patient working that lands on the right answer.
03Where it fits

Where it sits in the training story.

It comes after
The model already knows how to follow instructions. This step deepens its ability to think through hard, checkable problems instead of guessing on the first line.
Not the same as
Asking the model to "think step by step" in the prompt. That is a request at run time. This step bakes the habit into the model's weights.
04When it matters

When to take it seriously.

  • Your tasks have a clear right answer that a script can check (math, code, formal logic).
  • The model is fluent but rushes, jumps to conclusions, or invents intermediate steps.
  • You are happy to spend more compute at training time so the model thinks harder when it answers.

Sources
  • OpenAI (2024). Learning to Reason with LLMs (o1 system card). Trains the model to produce long internal working before answering, rewarded on problems where the answer can be checked.
  • DeepSeek-AI (2025). DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning. arXiv:2501.12948. An open recipe: reward correct final answers on math and code, let the model figure out the working on its own.
