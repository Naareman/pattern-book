Data residency

A user in Germany should be served by storage and models inside the European Union. A user in India by ones inside India. The rule travels with the data.

01What is it?

What is it?

Data residency is the rule that personal data stays inside a chosen region while it lives and while it is being used. The system reads the user's home region, then picks storage, models, and logs that all sit inside that region. The data is sent there, processed there, and the trail of that processing also stays there.

03Where it fits

How it relates to nearby ideas.

What it covers: the choice of region for each piece of the pipeline (where data is stored, where the model runs, where the logs land) tied to who the user is.
Sits next to: hiding personal details strips fields out of the payload. Residency is about the whole payload going to the right place. The two work together: scrub first, then route.
Quick test: if a regulator asked "where physically did this user's request and reply sit while they happened?", can you point to one region for every stop? That is the residency promise.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • You sell to customers in regions with strong residency rules: the European Union, the United Kingdom, the Gulf, India, China.
  • You work in regulated sectors like banking or health, where the contract pins data to a country.
  • Your enterprise buyers ask "where does our data live?" during procurement and want a clean, regional answer.
