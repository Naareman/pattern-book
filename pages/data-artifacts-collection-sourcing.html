Collection and sourcing

Deciding where the raw data comes from and writing down the terms it came with.

01The idea

What is it?

Every dataset starts somewhere: a website, a partner, a paid feed, a public archive, your own product logs. Collection and sourcing is the step where you pick those origins on purpose, and record who owns each one, how you are allowed to use it, and when you pulled it.

03Where it fits

Where it sits in the flow.

Collection is the very first step. It happens before anyone cleans the data, labels it, or feeds it to a model. Get the sources and their terms right here, and every later step inherits a clean record of where things came from.
04When it matters

When to care about it.

  • You mix data from many places, so you need to know which source caused which problem later.
  • Some sources have rules (a license, a contract, a privacy promise) that limit how you can use them.
  • The world changes, so you need to remember the date each pull was taken.
