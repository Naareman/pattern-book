Three dimensions of how systems organize their work.

01The Idea

What Is It?

An agent does not just answer a question. It runs a loop: it thinks about what to do next, acts by calling a tool (a search engine, a calculator, some code), and observes the result. Then it loops back and thinks again. When it finally has enough, it gives you the answer. This loop is called Think - Act - Observe, and it lets the AI solve tasks that would need many separate steps from you.

03A Real Example

Where It Matters.

The task
A user asks: "Book me a flight to Madrid next Friday and add it to my calendar."
One call is not enough
The AI needs to search flights, pick the best option, confirm the booking, and then write the calendar event. Each step depends on the one before.
The agent loop
Think: search for flights. Act: call the flights tool. Observe: three options returned. Think: pick the cheapest. Act: call the booking tool. Observe: confirmed. Act: call the calendar tool. Done.
Why it works
The AI carries information forward across steps, the same way a person would. No human in the loop needed for each step.
