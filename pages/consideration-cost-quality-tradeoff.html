Spending more for better answers

Bigger models, more sampling, and extra checks cost more. The question is how much extra quality you actually get.

01What It Is

More money buys more quality, up to a point.

The cost and quality tradeoff is the curve every team eventually plots: as you move from a small model to a large one, add a verifier, or sample several answers and pick the best, accuracy climbs and so does the bill. The curve flattens. The job is to find the knee, where each extra dollar still earns a meaningful gain in quality.

04When It Matters

When to reach for it.

  • The team is debating an upgrade from a cheap model to an expensive one for a feature with real volume.
  • Quality looks fine on demos but drops on long-tail inputs, so the choice is whether to sample more answers and pick the best.
  • A second pass (a verifier or judge) is on the table, and you want to know the price of the extra accuracy.
