User Experience · Where you meet the AI

Background / agentic surfaces

No chat to open, no panel to click. The AI watches what you are already doing and offers a small nudge when it thinks it can help. You only notice it at the moment it is useful.

01What is it?

Help that finds you.

A background helper runs alongside your existing tool and surfaces a small suggestion at the right moment, with no separate AI surface to seek out.

02An example

Start the reply and watch the nudge appear.

New message
Tosara@team.co
SubjectRe: Friday review

Hi Sara,

Thanks for sending the deck. I had a chance to look through it...

AI
Smart Compose
Tap Tab to accept "I had a chance to look through it and have a few thoughts."

No menu to open. The helper appears only when it has something useful to offer, and you can ignore it.

03Where it fits

How it relates to nearby ideas.

What it is
A surface that hides itself. The host app is the main thing; the AI only shows when it has earned the interruption.
Not the same as
A sidebar. A sidebar is always visible and waits for you to talk to it. A background helper stays silent until it has something to say.
Pairs with
Confidence checks. A background helper that nudges too often or with weak ideas trains people to dismiss it. It only works if the suggestions usually land.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • The work is something the user already knows how to do (writing an email, writing code, scheduling) and you just want to speed up the repetitive parts.
  • You can tell quickly whether the AI is confident enough that a suggestion is worth showing.
  • You want adoption from people who would never describe themselves as AI users: they just notice their tools got better.
