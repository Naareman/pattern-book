Retrieval (dense, sparse, hybrid)

Pull back the pieces most likely to answer the question. Match on shared words, or on shared meaning, or both at once.

01What is it?

Two ways to find the right pieces.

Retrieval matches the question against the document index by words (exact keyword match), by meaning (semantic similarity), or both at once, returning the pieces most likely to answer it.

02An example

Same question, two ways to look.

Question: how many vacation days do I get?

03Where it fits

Where it fits.

Runs after
The prep step. Search reads the small, tagged pieces that were prepared ahead of time.
Hands off to
Rerank. The first search pulls back a dozen plausible matches; rerank sorts them so the strongest go in front.
The choice
Words alone miss synonyms. Meaning alone misses exact codes and names. Running both and blending the lists is usually the safer default.
04When it matters

When to tune it.

  • Your users phrase questions in many ways for the same idea.
  • Some questions need exact terms (order numbers, model codes, drug names).
  • The right text is in your files, yet the model keeps answering from somewhere else.
