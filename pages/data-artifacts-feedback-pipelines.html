Feedback data pipelines

Catching the thumbs-up, thumbs-down, and edits users leave behind, and turning them into clean examples the system can learn from.

01The idea

What is it?

Feedback pipelines are the plumbing that catches user signals, joins them to the original request, and writes them back as new training or evaluation examples.

02An example

A thumbs-down becomes a training row.

A user rates an answer. Tap play to follow the signal through the pipeline.

1
Capture
User taps a thumbs-down on the bot reply. The click is logged with the request and reply IDs.
2
Join
The signal is matched to the full conversation: the question, the bot reply, the model version that produced it.
3
Clean
Personal details are stripped. Spam and accidental taps are filtered out so they do not poison the dataset.
4
Store
A row is written to the feedback dataset, ready for the next training run or evaluation review.
Ready.
03Where it fits

Where it sits in the flow.

A feedback pipeline runs on its own schedule, listening to live traffic. Its output is just another dataset, which then gets cleaned, labeled, and used for training or evaluation like any other data source.
04When it matters

When to care about it.

  • You have real users on a live product and want the system to get better from how they use it.
  • The cheapest, freshest training signal is sitting in your own logs and you are not catching it.
  • Quality reviewers need a steady stream of real failures to study, not made-up cases.
