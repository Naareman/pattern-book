Anti-patterns

Things that look right but break in production. Each one has a clean fix elsewhere on the site.

Stuffing every tool in the system prompt at scale
Loading a hundred tools into the model's context upfront so it can "choose" which one to use.
Why it breaks The context fills up fast, tool-selection errors increase with list length, and you pay for tokens on tools the current task never needs.
Fix Progressive Tool Disclosure ↗
Caching error or rate-limit responses
Storing a rate_limited or not_found reply in the cache just like a valid answer.
Why it breaks A transient failure becomes permanent. Every request for that key returns the error until the cache is manually flushed.
Fix Cache-key policy and lifecycle ↗
Calling everything an "agent" regardless of shape
Labeling a fixed sequential pipeline as an "agent" because it uses a language model somewhere inside.
Why it breaks The word "agent" implies runtime planning and decision-making. Misusing it sets wrong expectations and hides whether the system actually adapts to what it sees.
Fix Use the shape vocabulary: pipeline vs loop vs hierarchical ↗
Trusting a judge that knows which output it is judging
Feeding an LLM-as-Judge both candidate answers with their model names, or in a consistent order, and treating the score as neutral.
Why it breaks Brand and style cues leak through. The judge develops a systematic preference for outputs from larger or in-house models, invalidating the comparison.
Fix Anonymize before judging (strip markers, randomize order) ↗
Vibe-coding without specs or evals
Generating code, eyeballing the output, and shipping it with no written spec or regression tests.
Why it breaks There is no artifact to regress against. Every change is a fresh guess, and failures accumulate invisibly until the system breaks in production.
Fix Spec-Driven Engineering Workflow (page coming soon)
Benchmark gaming
Reporting scores on public agent benchmarks as evidence of real-world quality, when those benchmarks can be gamed with memorized solutions or overfit prompts.
Why it breaks High benchmark scores mask real failure modes. Users experience problems that the leaderboard never measured.
Fix Error-analysis-first on your own production traces ↗
