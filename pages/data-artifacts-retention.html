Retention and deletion policies

Setting rules for how long each kind of data is kept and when it must be deleted.

01The idea

What is it?

Data does not get to live forever. Retention rules set how long each kind of data is kept, and deletion rules say what triggers it being wiped (a date, a user request, a project ending). Writing those rules down once, then enforcing them automatically, is what keeps a system honest, lean, and compliant.

02An example

Three buckets, three rules.

A support team holds different kinds of data. Tap a bucket to see its retention rule.

Keep for
30 days
Long enough to debug a recent issue, short enough that an old conversation cannot leak years later. After 30 days, an automated job wipes the row.
03Where it fits

Where it sits in the flow.

Retention rules apply to every piece of data the system keeps: raw inputs, cleaned sets, model outputs, logs. They run as scheduled jobs in the background, deleting on their own so no one has to remember.
04When it matters

When to care about it.

  • You hold personal data and rules like GDPR give people the right to be forgotten.
  • Storage costs are creeping up because nothing is ever deleted.
  • A breach risk grows with every extra day of old data you do not need.
