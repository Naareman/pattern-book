Document Q&A

Ask questions about your own files. The AI finds the relevant parts and answers using them, with citations.

Stateless Retrieval Data & artifacts
01The Idea

What is it?

Document Q&A finds the few passages in your files that match the question, then has the AI write the answer from only those passages, with citations.

02See it Work

Watch the relevant passages light up.

Press Play to ask a question. The chunks that match your question glow, then the answer is built from them with citations.

Question "What is the refund policy for annual plans?"
Document chunks
Chunk A · Pricing.pdf p.2 Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Unused days are not refunded. ✓ 92% match
Chunk B · Pricing.pdf p.3 Annual plans are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, a prorated credit is issued. ✓ 97% match
Chunk C · FAQ.pdf p.1 To request a refund, email billing@company.com with your order number. Response within 2 business days. ✓ 88% match
Chunk D · TOS.pdf p.7 Users must agree to the terms of service before purchasing any subscription plan offered by the company. 62% match
Chunk E · Setup.pdf p.1 Download the app and sign in to get started. Your account syncs across all devices automatically. 14% match

03Patterns It Uses

The pieces inside.

Stateless Retrieval finds the matching chunks Data & Artifacts stores and indexes your files Single-Call Control builds the prompt with citations
04A Real Example

What actually happens to your file.

File split into chunks
A 40-page PDF becomes ~200 chunks of roughly 300 words each. Smaller chunks match more precisely; too small and context is lost.
05Tradeoffs

The price you pay.

lowhigh
Setup effort
Answer quality
Hallucination risk
The AI can only answer what is in your documents. If the relevant chunk is missing or poorly written, the answer will be wrong or empty. Chunk quality matters more than model size.
