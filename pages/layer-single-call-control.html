Single-call control

Getting the best answer from one message to the AI: no tools, no memory, just one well-asked question.

One answer

Different ways to ask the AI and shape its answer, all from one message.

How you ask the question
Few-Shot (show examples)Give the AI a few worked examples first, so it copies the pattern you want. → Zero-Shot (just ask)Simply describe what you want in plain words, with no examples at all. →
Getting it to think before answering
Chain-of-Thought (think out loud)Ask the AI to work through its reasoning step by step before giving the final answer. This often makes it more accurate. → Self-Consistency (best of several tries)Ask the same question a few times, then go with the answer that comes up most often. →
Controlling the shape of the answer
Structured output (a fixed format)Make the answer come back in the exact same tidy format every time (like a filled-in form), so software can read it without guessing. →
Getting it to double-check itself
Reflection (review and fix)The AI looks back at its own answer, spots weak points, and rewrites it to be better. →
