Layer 6

Single-call control

How do you get the best answer out of a single message to the AI?

Try these first, before tools or memory: they cost almost nothing and often give the biggest win.

What do you want to do?

What you want Use this
Get it to copy a style I show → Few-Shot Give it a few examples to copy. Get an answer by just asking → Zero-Shot Describe what you want, no examples. Get a hard problem right → Chain-of-Thought Ask it to think step by step. Get a more reliable answer → Self-Consistency Ask a few times, take the most common. Get the answer in a fixed format → Structured output Tell it the exact shape to return. Get it to fix its own mistakes → Reflection Ask it to review and fix its answer.
