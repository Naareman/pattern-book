Voice surfaces

You speak, the AI speaks back. Hands free, eyes free. The conversation has a rhythm closer to a phone call than a chat thread.

01What is it?

A conversation with no screen.

A voice surface converts spoken input to a question, generates a reply, and reads it back, so the entire interaction is hands-free and screen-free.

03Where it fits

How it relates to nearby ideas.

What it is
A surface, not a model. The voice itself is just the in and out. The brain behind it is the same kind of model that powers a chat window.
Pairs with
Streaming and turn-taking. Voice only feels alive if replies start within a beat and the user can cut in mid-sentence.
Not the same as
A chat window with a microphone button. A real voice surface assumes you cannot see the screen at all.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • The user's hands or eyes are busy: driving, cooking, walking, working with their hands.
  • The answer is short enough to listen to without rewinding. Long answers belong on a screen.
  • You want speed: speaking is roughly three times faster than typing for most people, so quick captures and questions land much faster by voice.
