Inline / embedded surfaces

The AI lives next to the work, not instead of it. You keep editing your doc, your code, or your email, and the helper sits in a panel beside it, aware of what you have selected.

01What is it?

The AI rides shotgun to your real tool.

A sidebar is an AI panel embedded inside another product (a document editor, an IDE, a CRM, a design tool). The main screen still belongs to the work. The sidebar can see what you have open and what you have selected, so a question like "make this shorter" or "explain this function" works without you having to paste anything in. Notion AI, GitHub Copilot Chat, and Cursor's chat pane all sit in this shape.

03Where it fits

How it relates to nearby ideas.

What it is
A surface that is one tap away from the work. The user does not change apps, they just glance sideways.
Sits next to
A chat window. Same conversation feel, but a chat window owns the screen while a sidebar shares it with whatever else the person is doing.
Pairs with
Context the host app passes in: the open file, the selected text, the current row in the CRM. The sidebar feels smart because of what it can see.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • The user already has a tool of record (an editor, an IDE, a CRM) and you do not want to pull them out of it to get help.
  • Most questions are about what is on screen right now (this paragraph, this function, this lead), not about general knowledge.
  • You want adoption from people who would never open a separate AI app, just because it is there in a panel they already use.
