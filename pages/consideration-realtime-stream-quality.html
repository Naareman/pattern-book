Checking quality as it streams

Score the answer while it is being written, so you can spot trouble before the user reads to the end.

01What is it?

A live read of how it is going.

When an answer is streamed, every new word is also a new chance to look at the whole thing so far and ask: is this still on track? Streaming quality checks run light scores against the growing text as it arrives. A short script can watch tone, look up named facts against a source, or sniff for repetition and drift. The scores update in place. The benefit is early warning: a low score during the third sentence is much cheaper to act on than a low score on the final paragraph after the user has already seen it.

03Where it fits

How it sits next to nearby ideas.

What it does
Reads the answer as it grows and gives a running score, so a problem is visible the moment it starts.
Pairs with
It is the input to clean stopping and to charging logic. A poor score on the meter is what gives the system a reason to cut, fall back, or refund.
Not this
It is for quality, not safety. Sniffing for harmful content mid-stream is a separate check with its own rules.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • Live answers where waiting until the end to score is too late.
  • Long, expensive replies where catching a poor one early saves the rest of the spend.
  • Multi-model or routed setups where you want signals to compare versions on real traffic.
