Logit Biasing

Add or subtract probability mass from specific tokens before sampling, to nudge the model toward or away from particular words.

01What It Is

A thumb on the probability scale.

A logit bias is a signed number added to a specific token's score before sampling, raising its probability or banning it entirely, giving you direct control over which tokens can appear in the output.

02An Example

Biasing a yes/no classifier.

Token probabilities at step 1
Without bias, the model might produce any token. With logit biasing, only "yes" and "no" can be sampled.
03Where It Fits

Where it fits.

Home
A way of shaping the output as the model writes it. The nudge is applied while the answer is generated, inside one call. It is a setting you pass with the call, not an instruction in the prompt.
Neighbor
Temperature dials the overall randomness up or down. Logit biasing nudges specific words. Constrained Decoding rules out whole groups of words. All three steer the model as it writes, inside one call.
In short: it adjusts the odds of specific words as the answer is written. No second call, no rule set, just targeted nudges, inside one call.
04When It Matters

When to reach for it.

  • Sorting into a small, fixed set of labels: block every word except your labels and the model has to pick one of them.
  • Hard-banning specific words is more reliable than asking in the prompt, which matters at high volume where even rare slip-ups add up.
  • Most hosted services offer it, and the common open-model serving tools support it directly.
