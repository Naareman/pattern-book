A thumbs-up or thumbs-down on each answer

A small rating sits next to every reply, so the person can say it landed (or did not) with one tap.

01What is it?

One tap, one signal.

Two small icons next to each reply: thumbs-up and thumbs-down. A tap is enough. On a thumbs-down, a couple of follow-up tags (off-topic, not true, too long) and an optional comment box let the person say a little more if they want to. The signal flows back to the team behind the product. Over time, it shows which kinds of answers are landing and which are not.

03Where it fits

How it sits next to other pieces.

What it is
A small control next to each reply. The person taps it. The product gets a signal.
Pairs with
A pipe that collects the taps and the optional comments, and a way to inspect them later. Those pipes and dashboards live elsewhere; this page is the control itself.
Not this
A long survey shown at the end of the session. That asks too much, too late. This catches the reaction in the moment.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • The product is new and you do not yet know which answers feel right.
  • You need real signal from real use, not just lab tests.
  • You want to spot a sudden dip in quality after a change to the model or the prompt.
