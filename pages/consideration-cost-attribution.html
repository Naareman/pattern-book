Cost attribution

Trace each dollar back to the part of the system that spent it: a model, a step, a feature, a customer.

01What It Is

Itemize the bill.

Cost attribution tags every model call with labels (feature, model, tier, step) so a spend spike becomes a specific question with a specific answer rather than one large unexplained number.

02An Example

One month of spend, broken down.

Monthly bill by feature $ 8,420
Chat assistant46%
Document search28%
Daily summaries17%
Other9%
Reading the bar: chat is almost half the spend. If the team wants to save money, that is the first place to look.
03Where It Fits

How it relates to nearby ideas.

Feeds it
The runtime emits a trace for every call. Attribution reads those traces, groups the cost, and shows the total by label.
Sits next to
The cost and quality choice. Once you see where the money goes, you can ask whether each feature deserves the model it is using.
04When It Matters

When to reach for it.

  • The monthly bill jumped and the team needs to point to a cause within an hour.
  • Different customer tiers pay different prices, so you need per-tier margin to make pricing calls.
  • One team owns the platform, many teams ship features, and each team should own the cost of what they ship.
