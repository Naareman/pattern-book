Generation

From a small seed, the AI produces content that did not exist before.

01What is it?

A small seed grows a new artifact.

From a short brief (a topic, a request, a few constraints), the AI produces a new artifact that did not exist before.

02An Example

One seed, three drafts.

Seed:
SeedLaunch tweet for a coffee subscription
Draft AYour morning routine called. It wants better beans, delivered fresh, no thinking required.
Draft BSkip the cafe queue. Roasted Friday, on your counter Monday. First bag on us.
Draft CCoffee that knows you. Pick a roast, pick a rhythm, and we handle the rest.
03Where it fits

How it sits next to other shapes.

Sits next to
Transformation. Both produce text the user reads. Transformation is anchored to existing meaning; generation invents from a small brief.
Quality trap
The answer is new every time, so there is no key to grade against. Quality has to be judged with rubrics, a second model, or a human in the loop.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • The user needs a blank page filled: a draft email, a starter outline, a first version of code.
  • Variety helps the user: many options to choose from, not one canonical answer.
  • The risk of a made-up detail is acceptable, or there is a clear review step before anything ships.
