Context assembly

Once the right pieces are found, they have to be laid into the prompt in a clear shape: instructions on top, pieces in the middle, the actual question at the end.

01What is it?

A clear shape for the prompt the model reads.

Context assembly builds the final prompt: instruction first, retrieved passages in a labeled list, and the user's question at the very end, trimmed to fit within the size limit.

02An example

Inside one packed prompt.

Prompt sent to the model about 1,200 words
Instruction Answer the user's question using only the passages below. If the passages do not cover it, say so. Cite the source name after each fact.
Passage 1 · Handbook page 12 Full-time staff receive 22 paid leave days per year, granted in half-day units, refreshed on each work anniversary.
Passage 2 · Handbook page 14 Leave must be requested through the manager portal at least seven days in advance for any block longer than two days.
Passage 3 · Holiday calendar 2026 The office closes from December 24 to January 2; those days do not count against personal leave.
User question (last) How many vacation days do I get, and how do I book them?
Why this shape: instructions first set the rule, source-labeled passages give the model something to cite, and putting the question last keeps it fresh in the model's mind right before it answers.
03Where it fits

Where it fits.

Runs after
Search and rerank. By the time assembly runs, the shortlist of pieces is already chosen; this step decides how to lay them out.
Hands off to
The model call itself. The packed prompt is the exact text the model will read in one go.
Watch for
Long prompts where the key passage lands in the middle. Models tend to skim the middle, so put the most important passages near the start or the end.
04When it matters

When to tune it.

  • Retrieved pieces look correct in isolation, yet the answer is muddled or skips the obvious point.
  • The prompt is bumping against the size limit and pieces are getting silently dropped.
  • Users ask for sources and the answer cannot tell them which passage a fact came from.
