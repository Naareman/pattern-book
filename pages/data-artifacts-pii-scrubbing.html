PII Scrubbing & Redaction

Removing personally identifiable information from datasets before they are stored or used for training or retrieval.

01What It Is

The idea.

PII scrubbing (removing personal information, meaning names, email addresses, phone numbers, national IDs, and similar private details) is the process of stripping or masking those values from a dataset before the data is stored in a training corpus or a retrieval index. The goal is to ensure that no individual's private details leak into model weights or surface in generated answers. Scrubbing runs as a data-preparation step: the raw records come in, a pipeline detects the sensitive fields, and the output is a clean version where PII is replaced by a generic token or a synthetic stand-in.

03Where It Fits

Taxonomy placement.

Sub-axis: L−1 Data. PII scrubbing is a dataset preparation step that happens before data enters a training job or a retrieval index. That is the defining axis: the lifecycle stage of the artifact that feeds the system.
Filing rule: data consumed by training or retrieval belongs in L−1 Data. PII scrubbing acts on that data before it is consumed, so it files here. The runtime reading of an already-scrubbed index is an L0 concern.
Cross-reference: the discipline of deciding access rules, audit logging, and compliance obligations around personal data belongs in C3 Privacy, Security & Governance (specifically its "Runtime PII-handling" category). L−1 owns the scrubbing mechanism on the dataset side; C3 owns the policy and runtime discipline.
Quick test: does it happen to the raw data before storage or training? PII scrubbing files in L−1 Data. Does it happen to a live request or response at inference time? That is C3 Runtime PII-handling.
04When It Matters

Who needs this.

Anyone who trains on or retrieves from data about real people. That includes customer support logs, medical notes, HR records, user-generated content, and web-scraped text. Compliance frameworks (GDPR, HIPAA, and others) often legally require it before training or indexing. Even without a regulatory mandate, scrubbing prevents a model from memorising and reproducing a real person's email address or phone number in its outputs.
