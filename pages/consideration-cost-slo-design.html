Setting speed and uptime targets

Write down the speed and the uptime the service promises, on paper, with a number. Then run against those numbers every day.

01What It Is

A promise the team signs.

A service target is a written commitment for the experience the team owns: how fast a request returns and how often the service is up. A typical pair is "answer within 2 seconds for 95 out of 100 requests" and "the service runs 99.9 percent of the month". The two numbers turn vague feelings ("it feels slow today") into a measurable promise the team can defend, review, and renegotiate.

Two targets, this month.

04When It Matters

When to reach for it.

  • The service is going to paying customers and the contract has language about speed or uptime.
  • The team keeps arguing about whether the system is fast enough, with no shared number to point to.
  • You want a clear bar for when to ship a risky change and when to slow down and stabilize.
