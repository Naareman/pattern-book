Layer 5

Knowledge & Memory

How the AI looks things up and remembers what you already said.

01The Idea

What is it?

An AI only knows what is in its prompt. So when you ask a question about your own documents, the system first searches a store of text chunks for the most relevant ones, then drops those chunks into the prompt. That is retrieval (also called RAG). Alongside that, the system keeps a record of earlier turns in the conversation and adds those too, so the AI always knows what was already said. That is memory. Together, they give the AI the right context to answer well.

03The Memory Side

How the AI keeps track of earlier turns.

Retrieval finds knowledge from documents. Memory tracks the conversation itself. Watch how a follow-up question works because the AI still has the first answer in its prompt.

04Tradeoffs

What you gain and pay.

lowhigh
Accuracy gain
Prompt size
Cost
Complexity
Retrieval lets the AI answer questions about your documents without retraining. The cost: every retrieved chunk plus every prior turn adds tokens to the prompt, which increases latency and API cost. Good systems rank carefully so only the most useful context goes in.
