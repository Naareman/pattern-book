Latency budgets

Give every stage of the pipeline a slice of the total time. If a stage takes more, another stage must give some back.

01What It Is

A budget, in milliseconds.

A latency budget is a number the user can live with (say, 2 seconds) split among the steps that lead to the answer: pull context, route the request, run the model, format the output. Each step gets a target. When one step blows its slice, the team knows where to look. The budget covers the slow days too, so the targets are usually set against the worst common case, not the average.

03Where It Fits

How it relates to nearby ideas.

Sits next to
The speed and uptime targets. The budget is the timer those targets are checked against.
Trades against
Trading time for quality. A tighter budget often means a smaller model, less context, or fewer checks.
04When It Matters

When to reach for it.

  • The product feels fast for one person and slow for another, and the team needs to pin the culprit step.
  • A new step is being added (a verifier, a rerank, a tool call) and you want to see whether the budget still adds up.
  • Voice or chat surfaces, where the user feels every extra second between sending and seeing.
