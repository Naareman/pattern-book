Latency-quality tradeoff

Think longer, sample more, run an extra pass: each tactic buys quality and spends seconds. Pick the right point on the line.

01What It Is

More thinking, more waiting.

The latency and quality tradeoff is the dial you turn at request time. Stream the first answer that comes out: fast, sometimes wrong. Let the model think step by step, sample several answers, and pick the best: slower, usually better. The same model can sit at very different points depending on how patient the user is and how much the answer matters.

03Where It Fits

How it relates to nearby ideas.

Sits next to
Splitting the wait across steps. The budget caps the total time. This tradeoff says how much quality you can squeeze inside that cap.
Builds on
Spending more for better answers. The cost and time levers often move together: bigger model, more samples, extra pass.
04When It Matters

When to reach for it.

  • The same feature serves quick chat and careful work, so two patience settings would help (a fast lane and a thorough lane).
  • Reasoning is on the table: chain of thought, sampling, or a second pass. Each one buys quality and spends seconds.
  • Voice and live experiences, where waiting past one second feels like a stall.
