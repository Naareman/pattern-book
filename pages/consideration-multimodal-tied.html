Keep the answer tied to what was sent

How to make sure the model's answer reflects what was actually in the image, audio, or document, not what it assumes.

Want the answer to only reference what is literally in the image? → Stay tied to what was actually shown or said↗
Want to screen images for harmful or sensitive content before showing a response? → Catch harm in images and audio, not just text↗
