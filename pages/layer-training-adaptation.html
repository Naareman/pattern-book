Model Training & Adaptation

How the AI is taught and shaped, step by step, before you ever talk to it.

01The Idea

What is it?

Before you send a single message, the AI goes through several rounds of school. First it reads an enormous pile of text from the internet to learn language (pretraining). Then it studies worked examples for a specific job (fine-tuning). Finally, humans rate its answers and it adjusts to match their preferences (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)). Each round builds on the last, making the answer a little better. Larger models may also receive specialised training to improve their raw reasoning ability before any of this happens.

03A Real Example

GPT-4, stage by stage.

📚
Pretraining
GPT-4 is estimated to be trained on trillions of words: books, Wikipedia, code, websites.
This gave it general knowledge and the ability to produce fluent text in many languages and styles.
🎯
Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
OpenAI staff wrote thousands of ideal question-and-answer pairs and trained GPT-4 to copy that style.
This is what turned a next-word predictor into a conversational assistant that follows instructions.
👍
Human Feedback (RLHF)
Human raters compared pairs of answers and chose the better one. The model learned to predict those ratings, then optimised for them.
This is what made it helpful, honest, and less likely to say harmful things.
04Tradeoffs

What training can't fix.

LowHigh
Cost
Time
Capability lift
Reversibility
Training is the most powerful lever but the slowest and most expensive one. Knowledge has a cutoff date; fine-tuning is costly to repeat; human feedback can teach overconfidence if rater signals are noisy. How you prompt the trained model still matters just as much as how it was trained.
05Inside This Layer

What each training stage is for.

Build the base model

Want to teach it language from scratch? → Pretraining Objectives & Data
Want to keep general ability but shift knowledge into a new domain? → Continued Pretraining
Want to track whether the model is improving during a training run? → Evaluation During Training

Tune it to behave

Want to tune it for a job using worked examples? → Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
Want to shape behaviour using human or AI preference signals? → Preference Optimization (RLHF, DPO, Constitutional AI)
Want to build native step-by-step reasoning into the weights? → Reasoning-Capability Training

Make it smaller or cheaper

Want to fine-tune without retraining the full model? → PEFT (LoRA, QLoRA, Adapters)
Want to compress capability from a large model into a small one? → Distillation
Want to blend two trained models into one without extra training? → Model Merging
