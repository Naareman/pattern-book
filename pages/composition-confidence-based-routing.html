Confidence-based routing

Use a confidence score to decide what to do next: keep the answer, send it to a stronger model, or hand it to a human.

01What is it?

Score first, route second.

After the first model answers, its confidence score determines whether to ship the answer or escalate to a stronger model or a human reviewer.

02An Example

Same setup, two outcomes.

The threshold is a knob. Higher means more escalations and more cost. Lower means more cheap answers and more risk of a wrong one slipping through.
03Where it fits

How it sits with nearby ideas.

Compare to
Cascading always runs the cheap model first then escalates if quality fails. Confidence routing can also pick the model up front based on a score on the question itself.
Pairs with
Often paired with a human review queue: low-confidence answers go to a person instead of (or before) the bigger model.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • A wrong answer carries real cost (support tickets, refunds, safety).
  • You have a usable confidence signal (log-probability, judge score, self-rating).
  • You want a clear knob to trade cost against quality at run time.
