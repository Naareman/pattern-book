Streaming safety

Put a safety check between the model and the reader, so a harmful sentence can be cut before anyone sees it.

01What is it?

A guard sits on the wire.

Streaming safety sits a fast safety guard in the token flow between the model and the user, so a harmful chunk can be swapped out or the stream ended before that content ever reaches the screen.

03Where it fits

How it sits next to nearby ideas.

What it does
Sits between the model and the user, watching each chunk and intervening before harm reaches the screen.
Pairs with
Pairs with safety training that shapes the model and with checks that run on the finished answer. A guard catches the rare slips that get past training.
Not this
Checking quality (drift, accuracy, repetition) is a separate signal. Safety guards focus on harm, on policy, and on data leaks.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • Public-facing products where a single harmful sentence on screen carries real cost.
  • Regulated spaces (health, legal, finance) where personal data must stay in the building.
  • Voice assistants, where there is no chance to delete a sentence the user already heard.
