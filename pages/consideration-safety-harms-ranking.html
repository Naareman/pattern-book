Name and rank the harms

Make a short list of the bad things this system could do. Rank each by how bad it is. The ranking is what tells the team where to spend safety effort first.

01What is it?

A ranked list of what could go wrong.

A harm taxonomy is a short, named list of the specific bad outcomes the system could cause. Each entry has a name, a one-line definition, and a severity rank. Severity is judged on how reversible the harm is, how many people it touches, and how much the model would have contributed beyond what a search engine already offers.

02An Example

A starter list for a general chatbot.

Severity · Harm · What it looks like in practice
Severe
Help with mass-casualty attacks
Step-by-step guidance on weapons, pathogens, or critical infrastructure attacks.
Severe
Sexual content involving minors
Any generation, description, or roleplay that sexualises a child.
High
Targeted harassment or doxxing
Writing abuse aimed at a named person, or exposing private contact details.
High
Confident medical or legal misinformation
A wrong dosage or a made-up legal deadline given as fact.
Moderate
Persuasive scam content
Polished phishing emails or fake reviews on demand.
Moderate
Stereotyping in everyday answers
Casual generalisations about a group inside an otherwise normal reply.
03Where it fits

How it relates to nearby ideas.

Feeds into
Drawing the line uses this list as input. Each named harm gets a rule: help, help with care, or stop.
Feeds into
Red teaming uses the ranking to choose what to attack first. The severe items get attacked the hardest.
Different from
A general ethics framework. This is product-specific and short enough to hold in your head while reviewing examples.
04When it matters

When to reach for it.

  • Early product design, so the team agrees on what counts as a safety bug versus a quality bug.
  • Whenever a new feature opens a new surface (voice, image, tool use), so its specific harms are added before launch.
  • During incident reviews, so a real failure gets sorted into an existing category or earns a new one.
