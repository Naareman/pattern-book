Pattern

Few-Shot

Put a few worked examples right in the prompt, and the model copies the pattern.

01The Idea

What is it?

Few-shot prompting is showing the AI a few examples of the answer you want, right before your real question, so it copies the pattern.

02See It Work

Toggle the examples on and off.

The toggle shows or hides the example pairs inside the prompt. Run it both ways and watch how the output changes.

Examples (in the prompt)
Example 1
"The concert was a total blast." → sentiment: positive
Example 2
"The queue took forever and the staff were rude." → sentiment: negative
Example 3
"It was fine, nothing special." → sentiment: neutral
Now classify this: "The food was incredible but the service let it down."
