Data quality and deduplication

Cleaning out broken, low-value, and repeated examples so the dataset is small, sharp, and honest.

01The idea

What is it?

Raw data is messy: broken rows, near-empty pages, the same story copied across a hundred sites. Quality and deduplication is the cleaning pass that drops the junk and collapses the copies. A smaller, cleaner dataset usually trains a better model than a larger, noisy one.

02An example

Spot the duplicates.

A web crawl pulled these news snippets. The same wire story shows up four times. Tap to clean.

keep"Central bank holds rates steady at 4.25%."
copy"Central bank holds rates steady at 4.25%."
copy"CENTRAL BANK HOLDS RATES STEADY AT 4.25%"
keep"Inflation eased to 2.1% in April, the lowest in two years."
copy"Central bank holds rates steady at 4.25 percent."
keep"Tech sector layoffs slowed for a third month."
6 rows. 3 are near-copies of the same wire story.
03Where it fits

Where it sits in the flow.

Quality and deduplication runs after collection and before labeling or training. Every later step inherits whatever junk you do not drop here, so it pays to be strict.
04When it matters

When to care about it.

  • You pulled data from the web, where the same article gets reposted on many sites.
  • You merge several internal sources and the same record can show up in more than one.
  • You are about to spend money on labeling or training: cleaning first saves both.
